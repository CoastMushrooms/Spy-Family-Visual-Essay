INTRODUCTION

In a world shaped by conflict and secrecy, adversity often isolates individuals in their attempts at survival. However, sometimes that adversity acts as a bonding agent, piecing people together. The anime Spy x Family explores this idea through the lenses of Loid Forger, a master spy, sent to carry out Operation Strix. His goal being to create a fake family and get close to a high level politician to prevent war from breaking out between the nations Westalis and Ostania. In his pursuit to create the "perfect family", Loid unknowingly marries Yor Briar, a seasoned assassin; adopts Anya, a telepath; and rescues Bond, a dog able to glance into the future. The fate of these nations are in Loid and his family's hands, will they measure up?

CORE THESIS

"Although the Forger family is built on a facade of deception, their bond is ironically more authentic than the 'normal' world around them. By examining Loid’s emotional repression, Yor’s social inadequacy, and Anya’s fear of abandonment, it becomes clear that their individual adversities are not just obstacles to be overcome, but the very traits that allow them to 'fill the gaps' in one another’s lives."

MISSION SUCCESS

Despite that the Forger family is initially built off a lie, it evolves into something far more precious than each family member anticipates. Anya’s fear of abandonment, Loid’s emotional detachment, Yor’s struggle with her identity, and Bond’s trauma initially isolate them. However, within the safety of their unexpected family, their unique struggles allow them to connect and love one another. Rather than dividing them, their shared experiences with pain and loneliness become the very foundation of their bond, allowing them to “fill each other’s gaps.” In this way, adversity stops acting as an obstacle, but as the glue that binds them together.
